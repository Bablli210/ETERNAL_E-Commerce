Scent finder — notes

Quiz mechanics, ranking, results, lead capture

1

Five questions, one per screenConversionShopify

Progress bar, one question per screen, big tap targets, Back always available; under a minute end to end. Shopify: page template page.finder.json; questions stored as metaobject finder_question (text, options, one tag per option).

2

Answers as image tilesConversionMotion

Moods and moments as pictures, not perfume vocabulary — "golden hour" beats "amber-woody". Motion: tile lifts on hover or tap; the screen slides left on answer.

3

Three matches with a score and a reasonConversionShopify

Results show three scents, each with a match percentage and one line explaining it ("you chose After dark + Warm skin"). Shopify: each option maps to a tag; results = All scents ranked by tag overlap, computed in the theme, no app dependency.

4

Trio sample CTAConversionShopify

The primary result action: "Try all three as samples for EGP [price]" — one tap adds three sample variants; secondary buttons buy the bottle. Shopify: cart/add.js with the three variant ids; automatic discount on the trio.

5

Email the results — after the value, skippableConversionShopify

"Send my matches" captures email or phone after the matches are shown and is always skippable — never a gate. Shopify: customer created with the match tags → segmented follow-up in Shopify Email or Klaviyo.

6

Entry pointsConversion

Linked from the nav, the hero, the home band, the collection tile, the product page ("Not sure?") and the mobile menu. Every dead end on the site leads here.
