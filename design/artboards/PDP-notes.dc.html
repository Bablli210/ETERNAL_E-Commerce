Buy box hierarchyConversionShopify

Line tag → name → signature line → rating → price → inspired by. The signature line from the tale does the emotional work in one sentence. Shopify: metafields signature_line and inspired_by; rating from Judge.me.

Sizes as variants, samples as real variantsConversionShopify

55 ml bottle and 5 ml sample are the two variants of the same product, so "try first" never leaves the page and inventory is tracked. Shopify: option Size with two variants, price per variant; exclude the sample variant from bestseller sorting if it skews it.

"Add a sample too" + add to bagConversionShopify

One checkbox adds a 5 ml sample of the same scent to a full-bottle order (credited back with a code) — the risk-reducer at the moment of decision. Shopify: the theme's product form adds the sample variant as a second line; Shop Pay button under the main button.

Delivery time, returns, cash on delivery and "Ask us on WhatsApp" directly under the button. The low-stock line renders only when inventory is truly below [n] — never fake. Shopify: inventory check in Liquid; wa.me link with the product name prefilled.

Top / heart / base, each with an image and one line drawn from the tale rather than perfumer jargon. Shopify: metafields top_notes, heart_notes, base_notes, notes_copy. Motion: the three columns rise in sequence on scroll.

The taleConversionShopify

The first 600 characters of the story with "Read the full tale" → article. This is where a new brand earns desire. Shopify: metafield story (rich text) and tale (article reference).

Occasion, season, longevity and sillage as icons and meters — practical questions answered without a table. Shopify: metafields occasion, season, longevity (1–5), sillage (1–5).
