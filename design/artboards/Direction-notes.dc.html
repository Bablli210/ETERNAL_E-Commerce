1

The direction in one lineConversion

“Salt, stone and golden hour” — Mediterranean minimalism with texture and warm light. It is chosen for conversion as much as taste: warm neutrals photograph product well, dark bands give the tales weight, and restraint keeps attention on the two things that sell — the bottle and the story.

2

Principles are rules, not adjectivesConversion

Each principle carries an “on the site” rule so the visual design and the theme build can be checked against it: one idea per screen, texture over decoration, one light source, one story per bottle.

3

Colour system: neutrals do the workConversionShopify

Linen ground, Paper cards, Night ink, Dune hairlines. Golden hour is the only warm accent (meters, index numbers, the bag badge); Deep sea is eterno’s tone and the hover colour. Shopify: theme colour schemes = Linen, Paper, Night, Deep sea, plus the three line tones.

4

Colour worlds per scentConversionShopify

One saturated colour per bottle, from the packshot seamless to the card, gallery, cart line and tale band. Two are fixed by your existing packshots; the others are proposals for packaging to confirm. Shopify: product metafield color_world (hex) read by the card, PDP and cart snippets.

5

Typography: Cormorant Garamond + Instrument SansConversion

The serif echoes the soft, calligraphic wordmark on the bottle and the editorial references; the sans is quiet and slightly narrow so prices, nav and buttons never compete with the headline. Alternatives if you want more edge: Instrument Serif (sharper, fashion) or Fraunces Soft (closer to the looped e of the wordmark).

6

Arabic pairingConversionShopify

Amiri for display, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for UI — both on Google Fonts, both render well on mid-range Android. Arabic runs 5–10 % larger with a taller line-height. Shopify: Translate & Adapt for strings; the theme swaps the font stack on the ar locale and flips the grid to RTL.

7

Type scaleConversion

Nine roles from Display XL to Eyebrow, with mobile sizes. Tabular numerals on every price and meter. Body at 17 px on desktop for the tales — reading comfort is a conversion lever on a story-led site.

8

MaterialsConversionMotion

Stone and sea for dark bands, sand for tinted sections, linen for cards, glass and water for the notes. Grain at 3–4 % on Night and Deep sea only. Motion in phase 2 treats these as light moving across surfaces, never as effects.

9

IconographyConversionShopify

20 px, 1.5 px stroke, round caps — the same set the wireframes already use. Shopify: inline SVG snippets in the theme, one file per icon, currentColor so they inherit the band colour.

10

Logo and the e∞ markConversionShopifyMotion

Wordmark on Linen for header and footer; mark inverted on Night for favicon, loader, empty states; a 6–8 % watermark on colour-world bands only. Motion: the mark is the loader and the finder’s “composing” state — a single stroke draw, never a spin.

11

Photography briefConversion

Five shot types with counts: packshots ×43, notes stills per family, editorial per line and per tale, scale and hands, abstract texture. All in the same hour of light so the site reads as one world. This brief is the shoot list for the campaign days.

12

Layout and shapeConversionShopify

12 columns, 80 / 20 px margins, text 5 columns and image 7, radius 0, hairlines instead of boxes, no drop shadows. Shopify: section padding and container width as theme settings; every section follows the same 120 / 72 px rhythm.

A1

Hero on the colour worldConversionMotion

The featured scent’s colour world becomes the hero background, so the first screen is already a product page in spirit. In production the film loop replaces the still and the packshot is its poster frame. Both CTAs sit above the trust line.

A2

Proof strip in display numeralsConversion

Large tabular serif numbers make the four facts read as editorial rather than as trust badges. They count up in phase 2 (H2).

A3

Index numbers and asymmetryConversion

Sections carry 01–04 indices, a left-aligned serif title and the link or button on the right baseline — the editorial rhythm from the BARE EARTH and Decanova references. Line tiles use the three line tones as their seamless.

A4

Bestseller cardsConversionShopify

Colour-world image, line eyebrow, serif name, tabular price, inspired-by line and the two actions. Shopify: featured-collection section; the card snippet reads color_world, inspired_by and notes_short.

A5

Dark tale bandConversionShopify

Deep sea with grain and the watermark; tale copy in Linen, the signature line in italic, two buttons. Shopify: the section takes a product reference and reads story, signature_line and color_world.

A6

Finder bandConversion

A single sentence and one button on Linen — deliberately the quietest band on the page, right after the loudest one, so the undecided visitor gets a breath and a path.

A7

Mood tilesConversionShopify

Six texture images with the mood name in the serif — no copy, no buttons; the picture is the argument. Shopify: collection-list section on the six mood collections.
