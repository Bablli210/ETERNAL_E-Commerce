Website wireframes

Shopify (Online Store 2.0) · desktop 1440 + mobile 390 · version 1 · 17 September 2026

The job of this site: convert first-time visitors who cannot smell the product. Every section on the boards that follow earns its place by doing one of three things — reducing the risk of buying blind, building desire through the tales and the notes, or removing friction between "I like this" and a paid order.

Mid-fidelity: real structure, hierarchy, copy and product data; imagery as labelled slots. Art direction follows the moodboard — Mediterranean minimalism, matière and golden-hour light — and is applied in the visual design phase after these boards are signed off.

Conversion architecture

Reduce the risk
why people hesitate to buy a scent online
5 ml samples as real variants, on every card and every buy box
Discovery set and mystery box with a full band on the home page
Cash on delivery and the delivery promise repeated at every decision point
"Inspired by" named plainly, with an honest "how ours differs"
Reviews with photos and longevity keywords; FAQ on originality and lasting power
Build the desire
why they pick eternal over the original or another dupe
One tale per scent (the PDF) surfaced on the PDP, the home page and the journal
Notes shown as imagery plus sensory copy, not a list of chemicals
Colour worlds per scent (destiny blue, caribbean punch cream) carried through cards and galleries
The house film and manifesto for an inspired-by brand that means it
Shop by mood for people who do not know notes
Remove the friction
why they finish the order today
Scent finder in the main nav: 5 questions, 3 matches, one-tap sample bundle
Search by the original you love, with synonyms
Cart drawer instead of a cart page; sticky add-to-cart on mobile
Shop Pay / express checkout, COD, phone-first checkout, Arabic
WhatsApp for pre-purchase questions and order updates
