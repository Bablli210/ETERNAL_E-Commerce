1

Announcement bar: one generous messageConversionShopify

One rotating message at most, and it is the risk-reducer (free samples with every order / free shipping over EGP [threshold]) — not a sale, so the first impression is generous rather than discounted. Shopify: announcement-bar section, one block, hides in the compact sticky state.

2

Header: four links, Scent finder at first levelConversionShopify

Shop · Scent finder · Tales · The house. The finder sits in the primary nav because first-time visitors cannot smell the product — it is the path for the undecided. Transparent over the home hero, solid elsewhere, compact sticky after 120 px so the bag is always one click away. Shopify: header section, sticky on scroll-up, logo as SVG.

3

Mega menu: three ways in + two featured tilesConversionShopify

By line (her / him / unisex), by family, by mood — the three ways people actually think ("something for a man", "something woody", "something for nights"). Two tiles push the bestseller and the discovery set. Shopify: mega-menu blocks; collections automated by tags line:*, family:*, mood:*.

4

Bag opens a drawer, never a pageConversionShopify

Count badge on the icon; add-to-cart opens the drawer in place so the shopper never leaves the product page (see the Cart row). Shopify: theme setting cart type = drawer; cart-drawer section with AJAX add.

5

Predictive search with inspired-by synonymsConversionShopify

Most shoppers search the original they know ("Blue Talisman"), not our name. Results group into scents / tales / popular, and the inspired-by name is highlighted in each result. Shopify: Search & Discovery — add each product's inspired_by metafield to a synonym group; predictive search API in the theme.

6

AR / EN switch, prices fixed in EGPConversionShopify

Arabic-first market: language switcher in the header and footer, currency fixed to EGP so there is no conversion surprise at checkout. Shopify: Translate & Adapt for Arabic, Markets with Egypt as primary; confirm the theme supports RTL before purchase.

7

Footer: newsletter + WhatsAppConversionShopify

The footer earns a second chance: email capture with the sample offer as the hook, WhatsApp click-to-chat for the many who ask before they buy, delivery and returns links. Shopify: footer blocks, Shopify Email or Klaviyo form, wa.me link or WhatsApp app widget.

8

Trust + payment stripConversionShopify

Payment icons (cards, Shop Pay, Fawry / Paymob / InstaPay / cash on delivery as enabled), "Delivery across Egypt in [n] days", returns line. Sets the expectation early so checkout is not the first place they meet a COD fee. Shopify: icons render from enabled gateways; copy from theme settings.

9

Mobile drawer: finder first, then the linesConversion

The mobile menu leads with "Find your scent" and the three lines as image tiles, then the rest as text rows. Language switch and WhatsApp sit at the bottom. Full-height drawer, 48 px rows, 44 px targets everywhere.

10

WhatsApp floating buttonConversionMotion

Bottom-right on mobile, above the sticky add-to-bag bar, hidden while the cart drawer is open. Motion: appears after 3 s, pulses once, never loops.
