Design direction · v1 · 17 September 2026

Salt, stone and golden hour.

Mediterranean minimalism with matière: warm ivory grounds, hairline architecture, one saturated colour world per bottle, photography lit at 4 pm. Quiet enough to feel expensive, warm enough to feel like Cairo in October — not a cold Scandinavian template.

2
Four principles
01

Quiet luxury

Nothing shouts. Fewer elements, larger, with room to breathe. The product and the word carry the page.

On the site: One idea per screen height; 120 px section rhythm; no badges, ribbons or countdowns.
02

Matière

Stone, sand, linen, wet glass, film grain. Texture replaces decoration — the site should feel like something you could touch.

On the site: Photography as texture; 3–4 % grain on dark bands; hairline rules instead of boxes.
03

Golden hour

One light source, low and warm, long hard shadows. Every image on the site could have been shot in the same hour.

On the site: Shot brief for all photography; amber as the only warm accent; ivory instead of white.
04

One story per bottle

Each scent owns a colour world and a tale. The catalogue is a set of worlds, not a grid of bottles.

On the site: Colour-world packshots, tale excerpt on every PDP, mood and family as the ways in.

4
Colour worlds — one per scent

Every bottle is photographed on a seamless in its own colour; the same colour tints its card, its PDP gallery, its cart line and its tale. Two are set by the packshots you already have; the other eight are proposals for the packaging team to confirm. The remaining 33 SKUs follow the same rule: pick from the world’s family palette, never a second saturated hue.

Texture — dividers, empty states, 404

Grid

12 columns · 24 px gutter · 80 px margins at 1440, 20 px at 390 · content max 1280. Text 5 columns, image 7 — the asymmetry is the signature. Section rhythm 120 px desktop / 72 px mobile.

Shape

Radius 0 on everything except inputs (2 px) and pills. No drop shadows — depth comes from light in the photography and from Night bands. Borders are hairlines in Dune.

Index numbers

Sections, notes and process steps carry a small tabular index (01, 02, 03) in Ash — an editorial device that also helps scanning.

Dark bands

Night or Deep sea, with 3–4 % grain and a 6 % watermark: hero, featured tale, house film, finder results. Never two dark bands in a row.
