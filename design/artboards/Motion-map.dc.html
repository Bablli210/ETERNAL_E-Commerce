Six rules
Light, not decorationMotion behaves like light moving across stone: slow reveals, fades from the colour world, one pulse. Nothing bounces, spins or loops in the reader’s eye line.
One thing at a timeA screen never runs two motions at once. The hero film is the only thing moving above the fold; the rest waits for scroll.
Earn the tapEvery motion tied to a conversion action is confirmation, not delay: Added ✓, the meter filling, the results composing. Never more than 1.2 s in the way.
Mobile is the primary deviceParallax, hover states and drifts are desktop-only. On mobile: swipe, snap, slide, fade — and the sticky bars.
Respect the switchprefers-reduced-motion turns every reveal into visible-by-default, pauses video on its poster and makes carousels manual. The site must read complete with motion off.
Never move the layoutOnly transform and opacity animate. Space is reserved for everything that appears later, so nothing shifts under the thumb (CLS 0).
