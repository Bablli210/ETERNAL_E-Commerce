Filters, cards, pacing and the bottom of the page

1

Collection header: descriptor, not a bannerConversionShopify

Title, one-line descriptor, count. No hero banner — the products are the hero and the first row stays above the fold. Shopify: templates/collection.json; descriptor from a collection metafield.

2

Family chips at first levelConversionShopify

Fresh · Woody · Amber & spice · Floral · Gourmand · Aquatic as chips — one-tap narrowing without opening a drawer. Shopify: Search & Discovery filter on metafield scent_family, rendered as chips by the theme.

3

Filter drawer + sortConversionShopify

Mood, inspired-by, longevity, price and season in a drawer; active filters shown as removable chips; sort defaults to bestselling. Shopify: Search & Discovery facets on metafields; sort options in section settings.

4

Search by the original you loveConversionShopify

A dedicated field on the collection page because "like X" is the dominant intent. Shopify: the same predictive search endpoint scoped to the collection, inspired_by synonyms as in Global 5.

5

Card = everything needed to decideConversionShopify

Bottle on its colour world, name, line, inspired by, three notes, price, rating — a full decision without a click. Shopify: metafields inspired_by, notes_short, color_world (file); rating from Judge.me.

6

Two actions per cardConversion

Quick add 55 ml and Add sample — the sample is the low-risk yes for a first-time buyer. Both open the cart drawer; the image and name link to the product page.

7

Editorial tile every eight cardsConversionShopify

A tale quote or the finder tile breaks the grid and re-engages scrollers; on mobile it spans both columns. Shopify: collection section with promo blocks at fixed positions.

8

Load more, not infinite scrollConversion

"Showing 12 of [n]" plus a Show more button keeps the footer reachable and the URL shareable; the count tells them there is more.

9

Discovery band + FAQ at the bottomConversionShopify

Shoppers who scroll to the end get the discovery set and three objections answered (longevity, originals, returns). Shopify: metaobject faq_entry shared with product pages.

10

Mobile: sticky chips + one filter buttonConversion

Family chips stay sticky under the header; filter and sort collapse into one button on the right; a two-column grid keeps the same two card actions at 44 px.
