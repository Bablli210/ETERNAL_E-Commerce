Voice rules for everything written
Second person, present tense“You can smell the ones the sea decided to give back.” Not “our fragrance offers”.
Senses before chemistryName what it feels like first; the note list comes second and in the pyramid.
Short headlinesEight words or fewer; one full stop; no exclamation marks anywhere on the site.
Honest about originalsName the original once, say how ours differs, never claim it is the same.
Arabic parityEvery English string has an Arabic one — written, not machine-translated; numerals stay Western in prices.
Placeholders die before launchEvery [bracket] on the boards is a line in this plan.
