Design direction · v1 · 17 September 2026
Salt, stone and golden hour.
Mediterranean minimalism with matière: warm ivory grounds, hairline architecture, one saturated colour world per bottle, photography lit at 4 pm. Quiet enough to feel expensive, warm enough to feel like Cairo in October — not a cold Scandinavian template.
2
Four principles
01
Quiet luxury
Nothing shouts. Fewer elements, larger, with room to breathe. The product and the word carry the page.
On the site:
One idea per screen height; 120 px section rhythm; no badges, ribbons or countdowns.
02
Matière
Stone, sand, linen, wet glass, film grain. Texture replaces decoration — the site should feel like something you could touch.
On the site:
Photography as texture; 3–4 % grain on dark bands; hairline rules instead of boxes.
03
Golden hour
One light source, low and warm, long hard shadows. Every image on the site could have been shot in the same hour.
On the site:
Shot brief for all photography; amber as the only warm accent; ivory instead of white.
04
One story per bottle
Each scent owns a colour world and a tale. The catalogue is a set of worlds, not a grid of bottles.
On the site:
Colour-world packshots, tale excerpt on every PDP, mood and family as the ways in.

1
The direction in one line
Conversion
“Salt, stone and golden hour” — Mediterranean minimalism with texture and warm light. It is chosen for conversion as much as taste: warm neutrals photograph product well, dark bands give the tales weight, and restraint keeps attention on the two things that sell — the bottle and the story.
2
Principles are rules, not adjectives
Conversion
Each principle carries an “on the site” rule so the visual design and the theme build can be checked against it: one idea per screen, texture over decoration, one light source, one story per bottle.
3
Colour system: neutrals do the work
Conversion
Shopify
Linen ground, Paper cards, Night ink, Dune hairlines. Golden hour is the only warm accent (meters, index numbers, the bag badge); Deep sea is eterno’s tone and the hover colour. Shopify: theme colour schemes = Linen, Paper, Night, Deep sea, plus the three line tones.
4
Colour worlds per scent
Conversion
Shopify
One saturated colour per bottle, from the packshot seamless to the card, gallery, cart line and tale band. Two are fixed by your existing packshots; the others are proposals for packaging to confirm. Shopify: product metafield color_world (hex) read by the card, PDP and cart snippets.
5
Typography: Cormorant Garamond + Instrument Sans
Conversion
The serif echoes the soft, calligraphic wordmark on the bottle and the editorial references; the sans is quiet and slightly narrow so prices, nav and buttons never compete with the headline. Alternatives if you want more edge: Instrument Serif (sharper, fashion) or Fraunces Soft (closer to the looped e of the wordmark).
6
Arabic pairing
Conversion
Shopify
Amiri for display, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for UI — both on Google Fonts, both render well on mid-range Android. Arabic runs 5–10 % larger with a taller line-height. Shopify: Translate & Adapt for strings; the theme swaps the font stack on the ar locale and flips the grid to RTL.
7
Type scale
Conversion
Nine roles from Display XL to Eyebrow, with mobile sizes. Tabular numerals on every price and meter. Body at 17 px on desktop for the tales — reading comfort is a conversion lever on a story-led site.
8
Materials
Conversion
Motion
Stone and sea for dark bands, sand for tinted sections, linen for cards, glass and water for the notes. Grain at 3–4 % on Night and Deep sea only. Motion in phase 2 treats these as light moving across surfaces, never as effects.
9
Iconography
Conversion
Shopify
20 px, 1.5 px stroke, round caps — the same set the wireframes already use. Shopify: inline SVG snippets in the theme, one file per icon, currentColor so they inherit the band colour.
10
Logo and the e∞ mark
Conversion
Shopify
Motion
Wordmark on Linen for header and footer; mark inverted on Night for favicon, loader, empty states; a 6–8 % watermark on colour-world bands only. Motion: the mark is the loader and the finder’s “composing” state — a single stroke draw, never a spin.
11
Photography brief
Conversion
Five shot types with counts: packshots ×43, notes stills per family, editorial per line and per tale, scale and hands, abstract texture. All in the same hour of light so the site reads as one world. This brief is the shoot list for the campaign days.
12
Layout and shape
Conversion
Shopify
12 columns, 80 / 20 px margins, text 5 columns and image 7, radius 0, hairlines instead of boxes, no drop shadows. Shopify: section padding and container width as theme settings; every section follows the same 120 / 72 px rhythm.

Motion spec · tokens and rules
Every animation, one line each.
Durations
xs 120 · s 200 · m 320 · l 600 · xl 1200 ms. Micro-feedback is xs/s, drawers and screens m, meters and counts l, the mark draw xl.
Easing
standard cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1) · emphasized cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) for drawers and screens · exit cubic-bezier(.4,0,1,1) · linear only for the reviews drift.
Distances and scale
Rise 16 px (24 on display type) · nudge 4 px · slide 8 px · hover scale 1.03 max · parallax factor ≤ 0.2 · stagger 40 / 60 / 80 / 120 ms.
Triggers
Scroll reveals at 20 % visibility, once; count-ups and meters at 50 %; sticky elements on the main control leaving the viewport.
Performance budget
Hero video ≤ 2.5 MB (mp4 + webm), poster ≤ 120 KB; GSAP only on templates that need it (home, PDP, finder), deferred; no animation on the LCP element except the poster fade.
Implementation
CSS custom properties for the tokens · a data-reveal utility on IntersectionObserver · GSAP + ScrollTrigger for H1, H5, D5, F3 · inline SVG stroke draw for the e∞ mark (no Lottie needed) · View Transitions API for G2 · native scroll-snap carousels · a global “Enable motion” theme setting plus prefers-reduced-motion.

Six rules
Light, not decoration
Motion behaves like light moving across stone: slow reveals, fades from the colour world, one pulse. Nothing bounces, spins or loops in the reader’s eye line.
One thing at a time
A screen never runs two motions at once. The hero film is the only thing moving above the fold; the rest waits for scroll.
Earn the tap
Every motion tied to a conversion action is confirmation, not delay: Added ✓, the meter filling, the results composing. Never more than 1.2 s in the way.
Mobile is the primary device
Parallax, hover states and drifts are desktop-only. On mobile: swipe, snap, slide, fade — and the sticky bars.
Respect the switch
prefers-reduced-motion turns every reveal into visible-by-default, pauses video on its poster and makes carousels manual. The site must read complete with motion off.
Never move the layout
Only transform and opacity animate. Space is reserved for everything that appears later, so nothing shifts under the thumb (CLS 0).
